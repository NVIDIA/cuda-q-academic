What happens next?

Finding ωr,|0⟩ is only the first part of calibration. Many other checks follow — for example, calibrating how readout distinguishes |0⟩ from |1⟩.

Those states change the resonator’s response in opposite ways, so the outgoing probe looks slightly different for each.

Real lab data

A sharp dip in how much signal comes through marks the resonator frequency with the qubit in |0⟩, written ωr,|0⟩, on real hardware.
