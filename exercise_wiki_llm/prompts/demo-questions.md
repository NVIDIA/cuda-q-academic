# Demo Questions

Ask these before and after ingestion. In the blank wiki, the agent should say
there is not enough local wiki evidence. In the complete wiki, it should answer
with citations to the wiki pages that contain the evidence.

1. Which ingested paper is the best evidence that CUDA-Q can support applied
   quantum chemistry workflows, and what practical limits did it report?
2. Across the wiki, what are the main bottlenecks for scaling GPU-accelerated
   quantum simulation?
3. What is the difference between using CUDA-Q state-vector simulation and
   CUDA-Q MPS or tensor-network simulation?
4. Which papers discuss multi-GPU or HPC deployment, and what infrastructure
   choices did they make?
5. What should a quantum researcher try first if they are converting an
   existing Qiskit workflow into a CUDA-Q workflow?
6. Which source gives the clearest warning about debugging or correctness risks
   in larger quantum circuits?
7. What open research questions remain after reading these papers?
8. Draft a short recommendation for a lab deciding whether to use CUDA-Q for
   VQE, large-circuit simulation, or hybrid QPU/GPU workflows.
