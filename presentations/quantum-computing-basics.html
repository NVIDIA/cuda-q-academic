Qubit Foundations

Five linked views: one qubit, tensor products, gates, interference, and entanglement.

Instructions
  1. Pick a topic tab. Each tab builds on the one before it.
  2. Qubit: set the state with presets or sliders, then measure to compare samples with probabilities.
  3. Two qubits: set qubits A (blue) and B (orange) and see their tensor product.
  4. Gates: apply single-qubit gates and watch the arrow move on the Bloch sphere. Then compare tensor-product gates with CNOT on two qubits, and measure the final state in the algorithm picture.
  5. Interference: compare H H with H Z H. Play each Bloch sphere, then read the two components of the state after every gate and see which outcome cancels.
  6. Entanglement: rotate qubit A, toggle the CNOT, and sample shots to see correlation.

Keyboard: Tab moves between controls; the focused slider shows a green ring. ← → switch tabs and move within button rows. Enter or Space presses a button. On a Bloch sphere, Tab to it and use the arrow keys to rotate the view. Esc resets the current tab.

A unit vector in ℂ². |0⟩ and |1⟩ are the two basis vectors above. Measuring returns 0 with probability |α|² and 1 with probability |β|², and leaves the qubit in the basis state it returned.
A bit is the special case α, β ∈ {0, 1}. Everything else below (the Bloch sphere, θ and φ) is just a way to draw a unit vector in ℂ² up to an overall phase.

The state

Any state is a weighted sum of the basis states |0⟩ and |1⟩. The amplitude α is the top entry of the vector and β the bottom one, with |α|² + |β|² = 1.

From gates to an algorithm

A quantum algorithm is a sequence of gates. Multiplied together they are one matrix acting on the whole state vector. The goal is to reshape the distribution so the answer you want is the likely outcome when you measure.
Two circuits that differ by one gate. Follow the arrow on the sphere and the two components of the state. The second H adds the components for outcome 0 and subtracts them for outcome 1; a sign flip in between decides which one cancels.
