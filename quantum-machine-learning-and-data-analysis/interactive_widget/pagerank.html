Read a PageRank update from the full matrices

Choose a page where the reader is currently located. Column j lists the probabilities of arriving at every destination page next. The same source column is outlined in each complete matrix, while highlighted arrows leave that source page in the network above.

P[:, A] = 0.85 L[:, A] + 0.15 T[:, A]

In every matrix, rows and columns are ordered A, B, C, D, E. Rows are destinations; columns are sources.

L — follow a link

T — teleport

The next page is chosen independently of the current page—like using a new tab, bookmark, search, or address bar. Here all five pages are equally likely.

P — combine both behaviors

With probability 0.85 the reader follows a link; with probability 0.15 they teleport. This is the column used in the PageRank update.

Why both? L preserves the web's link structure, while T prevents readers from becoming trapped on pages with no exits or inside closed groups of pages. Together they produce a stable ranking while keeping every page reachable.
