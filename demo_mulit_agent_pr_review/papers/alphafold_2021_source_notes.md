# AlphaFold 2021 Source Notes

Paper used for demo run: `Highly accurate protein structure prediction with AlphaFold`

Authors: John Jumper et al.

Primary source used: Nature open-access article, DOI `10.1038/s41586-021-03819-2`.

Source URL: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03819-2

Availability note: I did not find an arXiv version of this exact Nature paper. The article is freely available from Nature as an open-access article, so the demo uses that source instead of an arXiv PDF.

Source facts used in the title demo:

- The paper presents AlphaFold for highly accurate protein structure prediction.
- The paper frames the work as addressing the structure-prediction component of the protein-folding problem.
- The paper reports strong CASP14 performance.
- The paper reports a median CASP14 GDT score of 92.4, where GDT is a benchmark score, not a percent accuracy claim.
- The paper does not claim to solve every aspect of protein folding, replace all experiments, or make all protein biology predictable.
